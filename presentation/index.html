10 · DEVELOPER VIEW

Designed as a connected platform, not a collection of screens.

Domain Modules

Reservation, inventory, rooms, housekeeping, maintenance, POS, finance, CRM, revenue and more.

Cross-cutting Services

RBAC, workflows, notifications, audit/compliance, documents, integrations, multi-property and mobile access.

Integration Hub

REST APIs, webhooks, third-party connectors, sandbox environment and accounting integrations.

Business Rules

No overbooking beyond configured limits; no selling out-of-service rooms; auditable financial transactions; zero-balance protection.

State Management

Room, reservation, folio, payment, inventory and task states propagate to dependent modules.

Security

Role-based access, department access, audit logs, privacy controls and local-law/GDPR considerations.

Developer mental model: Domain event → business rule → state transition → downstream event → audit trail.
